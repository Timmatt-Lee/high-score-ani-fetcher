Privacy Policy for High Score Ani Fetcher

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

High Score Ani Fetcher (referred to as "this extension") highly respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data when you use this extension.

1. Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, browsing history, or sensitive information. All logic runs securely on your local device.

2. Data Storage

To provide its core functionality, this extension stores the following configurations locally using Chrome's Local Storage APIs (chrome.storage.local):

  • Your preference settings (e.g., minimum score thresholds, sorting choices).
  • Temporarily cached anime rating lists fetched from Gamer Anime Crazy (strictly to reduce redundant network traffic to Gamer's servers).

All the data mentioned above is stored exclusively on your local device and is never uploaded to any remote server or shared with third parties.

3. External Requests & Services

This extension only makes standard network requests to the official Gamer Anime Crazy website (ani.gamer.com.tw) to fetch rating details. We do not use any third-party analytics trackers, SDKs, or advertisements.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository page.
